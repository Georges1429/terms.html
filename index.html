Royal Air Force Bot

Terms of Service

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Purpose

The Royal Air Force Bot is a private bot created exclusively for the Royal Air Force server. It is used to manage member statistics, activity, sessions, attendance, recruitment, promotions, approvals, inactivity tracking, and other server-related functions.

2. Use of the Bot

Members may only use commands and features they have permission to use. Commands must be used for their intended purpose.

Members must not intentionally abuse, exploit, manipulate, or attempt to bypass any of the bot's systems.

3. Statistics & Activity

The bot records information related to server activity, including hosted sessions, attendance, recruitment, promotions, and other statistics.

Statistics may be viewed through /stats and may be modified by authorized personnel through administrative commands.

4. Approvals

Certain activities require leader approval before being added to a member's statistics. Leaders are responsible for approving or denying pending activities when applicable.

Attempting to falsely submit, manipulate, or approve activity is prohibited.

5. Inactivity

The bot monitors recorded hosted, attended, and recruitment activity for inactivity purposes.

Members may receive an inactivity notice after 4 days without recorded activity and a second notice after 10 days.

Posting in #inactive-notice pauses inactivity notices, and members with the designated exemption role are excluded.

The bot does not automatically change ranks or roles because of inactivity.

6. Administrative Commands

Administrative commands are restricted to authorized personnel. These commands can modify pending statistics or, in the case of /resetall, permanently wipe statistics for members.

Unauthorized use or attempts to gain access to administrative commands is prohibited.

7. Data & Records

The bot may store information necessary for its functions, including Discord user IDs, usernames, statistics, activity records, promotion records, and information submitted through the bot.

This information is used for Royal Air Force server management purposes only.

8. Accuracy

Members are responsible for providing accurate information when using the bot. False submissions or intentionally incorrect statistics may result in disciplinary action under the server's rules.

9. Availability

The bot may occasionally be unavailable due to maintenance, technical problems, updates, or other issues.

No guarantee is made that every feature will always function without errors.

10. Changes

The bot's commands, features, and these Terms of Service may be changed or updated when necessary.

11. Enforcement

Abuse, exploitation, unauthorized access, manipulation of statistics, or other misuse of the bot may result in removal of access to the bot and/or disciplinary action within the Royal Air Force server.

12. Discord

The Royal Air Force Bot is a private server tool and is not affiliated with or operated by Discord.
